# SECTION 16 — Executive Summary

The Karachi Division freight operation demonstrates strong earning capability and operational resilience, driven primarily by coal and container movement.

Although overall wagon count and tonnage declined compared to the previous year, freight revenue increased significantly, indicating stronger monetization efficiency and improved commodity mix.

The system remains highly dependent on a limited number of commodities and logistics partners, creating strategic concentration risk. Long-term sustainability will require diversification of freight categories, expansion of logistics partnerships, and modernization of operational planning systems.
